    Where the cards come from

    Every card on the left is a call I make in one of these four systems.

    Flexo

    Multi-tenant gym SaaS · 20+ paying gyms across India

    Members, plans, attendance and renewals for gyms across India. Every query is scoped to its gym, renewals are reminded on WhatsApp and paid through Razorpay, and each payment binds to the member's record. I own it end to end: schema, build, deploy and production support.

    Stack
    Next.js, Supabase Postgres, Prisma, Razorpay, Meta WhatsApp Business API

    Planorah

    My company · AI learning roadmaps · ₹0, ₹99, ₹249, ₹499

    A learner states a goal and the hours they have; Planorah returns a week-by-week roadmap. A Django monolith owns the product, Gemini 2.5 Flash drafts the plan in a FastAPI service, and a behavioural engine reshapes it around how that learner actually studies. Celery keeps the slow work out of the request.

    Stack
    Django, Postgres, Celery, FastAPI, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Razorpay, Nginx and Gunicorn

    Voice CRM

    White-label B2B · AI calls and WhatsApp

    A new lead gets a phone call, not a spreadsheet row. Tata Smartflo places the call, an ElevenLabs voice holds the conversation, the transcript is summarised back onto the lead, and a WhatsApp follow-up goes out. Nobody dials.

    Stack
    Python, Tata Smartflo, ElevenLabs, Meta WhatsApp Business API, Postgres

    Cybrik

    Day job · AI voice agents on WhatsApp

    At Cybrik Solutions I build agents that answer on WhatsApp for an immigration-services client: documents, visa stages and appointments, handled in conversation and passed to a person when it matters. Django and Flask services, LLMs through AWS Bedrock.

    Stack
    Django, Flask, AWS Bedrock, WhatsApp, Postgres
