Planorah

My company · AI learning roadmaps · ₹0, ₹99, ₹249, ₹499

A learner states a goal and the hours they have; Planorah returns a week-by-week roadmap. A Django monolith owns the product, Gemini 2.5 Flash drafts the plan in a FastAPI service, and a behavioural engine reshapes it around how that learner actually studies. Celery keeps the slow work out of the request.

Stack
Django, Postgres, Celery, FastAPI, Gemini 2.5 Flash, Razorpay, Nginx and Gunicorn
Unlocks
Task queues, LLM integration, Structured output
  1. Learner submits a goal
  2. Django validates it
  3. Job queued on Celery
  4. FastAPI service calls Gemini
  5. JSON checked against the schema
  6. Behavioural engine reorders
  7. Saved to Postgres
