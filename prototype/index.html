Selected work

Flexo

Gym SaaS · 20+ paying gyms

A multi-tenant SaaS that runs members, plans, attendance and renewals for 20+ paying gyms across India. Every query is scoped to its gym. Renewal reminders go out on WhatsApp, payments come in through Razorpay and bind to the member's record. I own it end to end: schema, build, deploy and production support.

Next.js · Supabase Postgres · Prisma · Razorpay · Meta WhatsApp Business API

Planorah

My company · AI learning roadmaps

An AI learning-roadmap platform I founded and build alone. A Django monolith takes a learner's goal, Gemini 2.5 Flash drafts a structured plan, and a behavioural engine reshapes it around how that learner actually studies. Celery runs the slow work and a FastAPI service handles generation. Plans cost ₹0, ₹99, ₹249 or ₹499.

Django · Postgres · Gemini 2.5 Flash · Celery · FastAPI · Razorpay · Nginx/Gunicorn

Voice CRM

White-label B2B · Calls and WhatsApp

A white-label CRM where a new lead gets a phone call, not a spreadsheet row. Tata Smartflo places the call, an ElevenLabs voice holds the conversation, the transcript is summarised back onto the lead, and a WhatsApp follow-up goes out through Meta's API. Nobody dials.

Python · Tata Smartflo · ElevenLabs · Meta WhatsApp Business API · Postgres

Why backends

I'm Abhinav, a backend engineer in Ludhiana. Most of what I build sits behind a payment, a phone call or a WhatsApp message, the three things small Indian businesses actually run on.

Flexo is the clearest example. It runs members, plans, attendance and renewals for more than twenty gyms, and I own every part of it, from the Postgres schema to production support.

By day I'm at Cybrik Solutions, building GenAI services on AWS Bedrock. Alongside it I'm building Planorah and finishing my MCA.

What I care about is the unglamorous part done properly: tenant data that never leaks, webhooks that are verified, retries that never charge anyone twice.
